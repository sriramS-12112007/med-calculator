Our story

Smart Medical Calculator started as a simple frustration: the formulas every student and clinician needs — eGFR, MAP, corrected sodium, tidal volume — are scattered across textbooks, old lecture slides, and a dozen different apps, each with its own quirks.

We built one focused toolkit instead: 60 calculators across six specialties, each showing the formula it's based on so you're never just trusting a black box. No sign-up required to use it, no ads, no clutter — just the number you came for, in seconds.

It's maintained as a side project by people who use these formulas themselves, and it's free to use for anyone studying or working in healthcare.

A note on clinical use

Smart Medical Calculator is an educational and decision-support tool. It is not a substitute for clinical judgment, and results should always be interpreted in the context of the full patient picture by a qualified professional.
