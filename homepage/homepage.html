About this calculator

Clinical math, done in seconds.

Smart Medical Calculator is a free set of bedside formulas for cardiology, respiratory, hematology, renal, neurology, and general health — built so students and healthcare professionals can go from patient values to a clinical number without reaching for a textbook or a spreadsheet.

Pick a specialty below to open its dashboard, or star the ones you use most and pull them up instantly from your Favorites dropdown up top — no digging through menus mid-shift. Every result comes with the formula it's based on, so you can check the working, not just the number.

Every specialty, one toolkit

Each category groups ten focused calculators, color-coded so you always know where you are. Tap a card to open it, or open any specialty and tap the star on a calculator to save it to Favorites.
