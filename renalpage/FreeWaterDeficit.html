Free Water Deficit

Estimates the volume of free water needed to correct hypernatremia, based on total body water and how far serum sodium has risen above normal.

Enter values

Formula

FWD (L) = TBW × [(Serum Na ÷ 140) − 1]

Total body water (TBW) is estimated as a fraction of body weight; this equation is intended for hypernatremia (serum Na above 140 mEq/L).

  • Adrogüe HJ, Madias NE. "Hypernatremia." N Engl J Med. 2000;342(20):1493–1499.

Worked Example

Weight 70 kg, Na 155 mEq/L, Male

(0.6 × 70) × [(155/140)−1] = 4.5 L deficit
